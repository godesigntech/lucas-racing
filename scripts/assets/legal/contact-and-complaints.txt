## Help with an order

Please give your order number if available, the item involved and a description of what you need. For a problem with a product or personalisation, photographs can help us understand it. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or payment information.

The seller will investigate and explain the next step. If the issue needs input from Delta Racewear or another fulfilment partner, the seller will arrange that while remaining responsible for its obligations to you.

## Complaints

Send your complaint to the relevant seller using the contact details above. Please explain what happened and the outcome you are seeking. We will respond as promptly as reasonably possible, ask for any information needed to investigate, and explain the proposed resolution.

If the complaint remains unresolved after our process, the seller will explain its position and provide information about any applicable alternative dispute resolution arrangement and whether it is required or willing to use it. Your statutory rights and access to the courts remain in force.
