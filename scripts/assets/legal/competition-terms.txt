## How to enter

Create a helmet design in the Lucas Racing website design studio and include at least one hydrographic film. Complete the entry through the competition option on the design review step and provide the requested link or entry evidence.

The social sharing requirement is [CONFIRM POST OR STORY REQUIREMENT REQUIRED TAGS AND HOW LONG THE ENTRY MUST REMAIN VISIBLE].

One entry is allowed per person. You may update your design until entries close. Your entry must use artwork you are entitled to submit and reproduce. We will explain any issue that prevents an entry meeting the published requirements and deal with entries consistently.

## Voting and selection

Eligible designs will be displayed on the competition's Facebook page without entrants' names attached. Each eligible entrant receives one vote and may vote for another entrant's design. Duplicate entries or votes and votes using another person's identity will be excluded where verified.

The design receiving the highest number of valid votes wins first prize. If first place is tied, the published judges are Howard Lucas, Kat Franklin and Mark Mitchel. The judging criteria and arrangements for ties affecting other ranked prizes are [CONFIRM TIE CRITERIA JUDGING ARRANGEMENTS AND RANKING OF OTHER PRIZES].

We will supervise and record the process so that prizes are awarded in accordance with these rules. Any voting verification will use only information reasonably needed for that purpose.

## Prizes and vouchers

- First prize is a new Arai SK-6 finished in the winning design, with a spoiler and clear visor. UK postage is included.
- Second place receives 50 percent off a custom helmet finish.
- Third place receives 40 percent off a custom helmet finish.
- Fourth place receives 30 percent off a custom helmet finish.
- Fifth place receives 20 percent off a custom helmet finish.
- Every other eligible entrant receives 10 percent off a custom helmet finish.

Vouchers are valid for 12 months from their issue date. The voucher code will be available in the entrant's website account under Competition and can be used when ordering the eligible custom helmet finish.

The eligible finish price, included options, any minimum spend, excluded costs, transfer rules and whether vouchers combine with another offer are [CONFIRM FULL VOUCHER CONDITIONS AND WHICH SELLER REDEEMS THEM]. All applicable restrictions must be stated before entry and purchase.

## Award and fulfilment

The winner will be contacted through [CONFIRM WINNER CONTACT METHOD]. The response period, information needed to arrange the prize and fair process if a winner cannot be contacted are [CONFIRM PRIZE CLAIM PROCESS AND DEADLINE].
