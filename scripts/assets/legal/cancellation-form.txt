# Cancellation form

Use this form if you wish to exercise a right to cancel an order. You can also cancel by sending any other clear statement. Completing this form is optional. The cancellation rights that apply to your goods or services are explained in our [Terms and Conditions](terms-and-conditions.html) and [Delivery Returns and Cancellations](delivery-returns-and-cancellations.html).
