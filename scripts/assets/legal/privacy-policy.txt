- Competition entries, voting information and prize or voucher records where you take part.
- Marketing preferences and any consent you give for publication of identifiable images or designs.

## Purposes and lawful grounds

We use information needed to answer a requested quote, accept and fulfil an order, arrange personalisation, take payment and deliver goods on the basis of taking steps at your request before a contract or performing the contract with you. If you do not provide information necessary for those steps, we may be unable to process the order and will explain what is needed.

We keep transaction and accounting information to meet legal obligations. We may also process information to respond to legal requests where the law requires it.

We use relevant information to keep the website and accounts secure, prevent fraud, resolve enquiries and complaints, and establish or defend legal claims on the basis of our legitimate interests, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. Those interests are operating the service reliably, protecting customers and managing business disputes fairly.

Where we rely on consent, including for marketing or optional tracking that requires it, we will explain the choice and how you can withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out. A privacy notice acknowledgement is separate from consent to marketing.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access your personal information, correct it, erase it, restrict its use, or obtain a portable copy. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, and you have an absolute right to object to use of your information for direct marketing.

Contact the responsible business using the details above. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity or understand the request and will respond within the applicable legal time limits. Some rights have legal exceptions, including where records must be kept to meet an obligation or deal with a claim.

Marketing messages will explain how to unsubscribe. You can also contact us to change your preference. Service messages about an existing order are sent where necessary to deal with that order.
