### Helmet booking and stage payments

The booking deposit applies to helmet orders. It is 20 percent of the agreed custom finishing price. If HydroKarts supplies a new helmet for your custom order, the initial payment also includes the full agreed price of that helmet. Your quote or checkout will show the helmet price and finishing charge separately, together with the payment schedule, before you commit.

A slot in the helmet production diary is booked only after the required initial payment has been received and we have confirmed your booking. We will explain the expected start date and any requirements for receiving your helmet or approving the design.

A further payment equal to 50 percent of the balance remaining after the required initial payment is due on the production start date. We will confirm that date with you. The final balance is invoiced when the helmet is completed and must be paid within 14 days of the completion notice and final invoice, and before collection or dispatch. This provision is subject to any legal right you have to withhold payment.

You may pay any instalment or the full balance early. Early payments are credited against the next scheduled amount due, and each invoice will show payments already received and what remains payable.

For example, if a new helmet costs £600 and the custom finish costs £1,000, the total price is £1,600. The payment schedule is as follows.

- £800 to book the slot, comprising the £600 helmet and £200 towards the finish.
- £400 on the production start date, which is half of the £800 remaining after the required booking payment.
- £400 on completion, payable within 14 days and before collection or dispatch.

Other products follow the payment arrangements stated at their checkout or in the agreed quote. The helmet booking and stage-payment schedule applies only to helmets.
