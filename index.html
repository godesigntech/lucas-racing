How it works

From idea to race day in four steps

  1. 1

    Choose your helmet

    Pick the Arai SK6 or Bell KC7. Buy a new one in your size, or send us your own.

  2. 2

    Pick a template

    Start from one of our racing-line templates, or draw your own from a blank helmet.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Colours, films, chrome, names, numbers, logos, rubber and plastic trims, and a spoiler.

  4. 4

    Approve & order

    Check it in 360°, save or share it, then order. We dip, chrome and finish it in-house.

About the team

Built by racers, for racers.

Lucas Racing is a UK karting team and the exclusive UK distributor of MS Kart. Our chassis and our race-day know-how help drivers go faster, get on the podium and stay there.

Hydro Karts is now part of the team. The twins behind it hydro dip and chrome race helmets in-house. They bring the custom side of the business, and Lucas Racing brings the team, the parts and the paddock experience.
